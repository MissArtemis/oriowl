最终提示词：

Use case: background-extraction. Edit target: the supplied OwlTrace brand artwork. Create a transparent square app logo asset by isolating ONLY the existing cute turquoise owl holding its photo/map card. Keep the owl's exact identity, pose, cream face and belly, two dark teal eyes with white highlights, orange beak and feet, aqua body, dark teal outline, proportions, and friendly illustration style as in the source. Preserve the card held between its wings. Remove the entire map platform beneath the owl, the route and map pins, all white background, all lettering and wordmarks, and corner watermark. Do not redesign or add elements. The owl itself must be unchanged as faithfully as possible. Center the complete owl with both feet and ears intact and about 10% transparent padding all around, balanced square composition, suitable for a crisp small mobile app icon. Output actual transparent PNG alpha, no colored backdrop, no shadow, no text.
